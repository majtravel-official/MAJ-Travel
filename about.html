Mark Jones — Founder & CEO of MAJ Travel

Professional itinerary design and customer‑focused travel solutions for the UK market.

I established MAJ Travel after relocating to Greater Manchester, bringing together my passion for UK travel with a clear commercial vision: to provide high‑quality, ready‑to‑run day excursions that help organisations expand their offering, strengthen customer engagement, and operate more efficiently.

With deep roots in North Wales — where I grew up exploring its landscapes, heritage towns, and coastline — I bring first‑hand regional knowledge that adds authenticity and value to every itinerary I design. This local insight, combined with a structured, professional approach, allows me to create day trips that are both commercially viable and genuinely memorable for customers.

MAJ Travel operates in partnership with Westfield Minibuses, combining trusted transport services with a growing portfolio of leisure excursions. Alongside our public trips, I specialise in B2B itinerary development for travel companies, tour operators, community groups, and transport providers.

What We Provide

  • Fully planned and timed day‑trip itineraries
  • Route planning and destination sequencing
  • Customer‑ready excursion packs and destination notes
  • Optional transport solutions through our partner network

It’s a cost‑effective way for organisations to introduce new excursions, refresh their programme, or expand into new regions without the internal workload of planning, testing, and structuring trips themselves.

My focus is simple: to deliver well‑designed, reliable, and engaging excursions that help businesses grow — while ensuring customers enjoy experiences that feel welcoming, accessible, and memorable.
